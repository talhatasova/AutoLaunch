# Application

The founder-facing workspace for preparing a product and choosing directory targets.

## Language

**Founder**:
A person who owns or represents one or more SaaS products and authorizes their directory submissions.
_Avoid_: Client, account

**Product**:
The founder's SaaS offering whose details can be sent to directories.
_Avoid_: App

**Directory**:
A third-party website that accepts product listings.

**Catalog entry**:
The curated record of a directory's submission path, price, conditions, and verification evidence.

**Eligible target**:
A catalog entry for a free directory whose complete submission path has been verified to work without a human step. A required backlink or badge is disclosed before selection.

**Listing obligation**:
A non-monetary condition, such as a backlink or badge, that a directory requires before or after accepting a listing.

**Submission selection**:
The set of eligible targets a founder approves for one product after reviewing the details each will receive.
_Avoid_: Launch
