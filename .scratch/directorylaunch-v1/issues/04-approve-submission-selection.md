# 04: Approve a submission selection

**What to build:** A founder chooses up to two eligible targets for a product, reviews the exact fields sent to each, grants directory-specific consent, and queues immutable submissions.

**Blocked by:** 02: Create a reviewed product; 03: Browse a truthful directory catalog

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Approved fields, target rules, and consent are stored as a snapshot.
- [ ] A backlink or badge target waits for confirmation that it is installed.
- [ ] Duplicate requests and over-limit selections do not enqueue another send.
